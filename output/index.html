Frequently Asked Questions

Is Rave Bingo gambling?

No. Rave Bingo is an interactive entertainment show with bingo-inspired rounds, music trivia, crowd challenges, dance-offs, and promotional giveaways. There is no wagering, no betting, and no cash bingo. Tickets purchase admission to the entertainment experience — not a chance to win money.

What am I paying for when I buy a ticket?

Your ticket is admission to the show: the DJ, the host, the interactive rounds, and the party. Prize promotions are separate from your purchase — buying a ticket or any add-on never buys you a chance at a sweepstakes prize.

How does the purple Vacation Bingo work?

Purple Vacation Bingo is a free, no-purchase-necessary promotional sweepstakes. Every eligible guest gets one free purple card. Purple cards are never sold and there are no paid extras for this game. You can also request free entry through the Winner's Circle without attending as a ticketed guest, where offered.

What is the Party Upgrade Package?

The Party Upgrade Package is an optional gameplay add-on that unlocks extra bonus game opportunities, enhanced prize experiences, collectible game materials, and additional interactive challenges. It's for fun and small promotional giveaways only — it cannot win and does not affect the purple Vacation Bingo prize in any way.

How are stage-game winners decided?

Stage-game prizes are won through actual skill games — the rules and scoring are announced before each game, and winners are determined by performance, scoring, or judging. Contestants can come from free Winner's Circle applications, volunteers, host/audience participation, skill qualifiers, and Party Upgrade Package qualifiers.

What is the Winner's Circle?

A free membership program. Members can receive free comp-entry passes and a free Party Upgrade Package at eligible events, and can apply to be stage-game contestants. Join free here — no purchase necessary.

What about sponsor prizes and travel certificates?

Some prizes are provided by third-party sponsors and are subject to the sponsor's own terms, conditions, availability, blackout dates, and redemption requirements. Rave Bingo and IRL Events are not responsible for sponsor fulfillment. See the Prize & Promotions Policy for details.
