How Prizes Work

Rave Bingo is a ticketed interactive entertainment show — not gambling and not paid bingo-for-cash. Your ticket buys the entertainment experience: the DJ, the host, the rounds, the chaos. Prizes come in three clearly separated lanes.

🏝️

Purple Vacation Bingo — Always Free

Our featured vacation giveaway runs as a free, no-purchase-necessary sweepstakes. Every eligible guest receives one free purple Vacation Bingo card. Purple cards are never sold, and there are no paid extras, upgrades, or add-ons for this game.

Buying a ticket, bonus cards, drinks, or anything else does not improve your chances. A free entry route is also available through the WINNERS CIRCLE.

🎤

Stage Games — Won by Skill

Stage-game prizes are won through actual skill challenges — trivia, dance-offs, lyric battles, and other contests with rules and scoring announced before each game. Winners are determined by performance, scoring, or judging, never by chance.

Contestants can be selected from free WINNERS CIRCLE applications, volunteers, host and audience participation, skill qualifiers, and bonus-card qualifiers.

🎁

Bingo-Round Giveaways — Just for Fun

Music-bingo round prizes are small, promotional, fun items — merch, sponsor swag, drink vouchers, bragging rights. They're played on cards included with the show or on free cards, separate from any purchase.

Optional bonus cards are gameplay add-ons for extra entertainment rounds only. Bonus cards cannot win and do not affect the purple Vacation Bingo prize.

No purchase necessary to enter or win any random-draw or sweepstakes prize. Rave Bingo is not gambling — there is no wagering, no betting, and no cash bingo. Sponsor-provided prizes and certificates are subject to sponsor terms. Read the full Prize & Promotions Policy →

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Rave Bingo gambling?

No. Rave Bingo is an interactive entertainment show with bingo-inspired rounds, music trivia, crowd challenges, dance-offs, and promotional giveaways. There is no wagering, no betting, and no cash bingo. Tickets purchase admission to the entertainment experience — not a chance to win money.

What am I paying for when I buy a ticket?

Your ticket is admission to the show: the DJ, the host, the interactive rounds, and the party. Prize promotions are separate from your purchase — buying a ticket or any add-on never buys you a chance at a sweepstakes prize.

How does the purple Vacation Bingo work?

Purple Vacation Bingo is a free, no-purchase-necessary promotional sweepstakes. Every eligible guest gets one free purple card. Purple cards are never sold and there are no paid extras for this game. You can also request free entry through the WINNERS CIRCLE without attending as a ticketed guest, where offered.

What are bonus cards?

Bonus cards are optional gameplay add-ons that let you play extra entertainment rounds. They are for fun and small promotional giveaways only — bonus cards cannot win and do not affect the purple Vacation Bingo prize in any way.

How are stage-game winners decided?

Stage-game prizes are won through actual skill games — the rules and scoring are announced before each game, and winners are determined by performance, scoring, or judging. Contestants can come from free WINNERS CIRCLE applications, volunteers, host/audience participation, skill qualifiers, and bonus-card qualifiers.

What is the WINNERS CIRCLE?

A free membership program. Members can receive free comp-entry passes and a free bonus pack at eligible events, and can apply to be stage-game contestants. Join free here — no purchase necessary.

What about sponsor prizes and travel certificates?

Some prizes are provided by third-party sponsors and are subject to the sponsor's own terms, conditions, availability, blackout dates, and redemption requirements. Rave Bingo and IRL Events are not responsible for sponsor fulfillment. See the Prize & Promotions Policy for details.
