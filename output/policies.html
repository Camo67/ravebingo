Prize & Promotions Policy

Rave Bingo · IRL Events · Last updated July 2026

The short version: Rave Bingo is an interactive entertainment show with bingo-inspired rounds, music trivia, crowd challenges, dance-offs, and promotional giveaways. It is a ticketed entertainment experience — not gambling and not paid bingo-for-cash. No purchase is ever necessary to enter or win any random-draw prize, and a purchase never improves your chances.

1. Tickets Are for the Entertainment Experience

  • Event tickets purchase admission to the entertainment experience: the live show, DJ, host, interactive rounds, music trivia, crowd challenges, and dance-offs.
  • A ticket is not a wager, a bet, an entry fee for a chance-based prize, or a purchase of a chance to win money.
  • No portion of any ticket price is a stake, pot, or jackpot contribution.

2. Purple Vacation Bingo — Official Free Sweepstakes Rules

  • Free game. Purple Vacation Bingo is a free promotional sweepstakes. NO PURCHASE NECESSARY to enter or win.
  • One purple card per eligible guest. Each eligible guest receives one free purple Vacation Bingo card at eligible events.
  • Never sold. Purple cards are never sold, and there are no paid extras, upgrades, additional cards, or add-ons of any kind for this game.
  • Purchases don't help. Buying a ticket, a Party Upgrade Package, food, drinks, merchandise, or anything else does not affect eligibility and does not increase your chances of winning.
  • Free entry route. A free alternate entry route is available through the free WINNERS CIRCLE membership program, where offered, without purchasing a ticket.
  • Eligibility. Guests must meet venue age and admission requirements. Employees, hosts, and event staff of Rave Bingo / IRL Events are not eligible. Void where prohibited by law.
  • Full per-event rules, prize descriptions, approximate retail values, odds statements, and winner-selection procedures are announced at the event and/or posted at the event page for that event.

3. Party Upgrade Package — Optional Gameplay Add-On

  • The Party Upgrade Package is an optional gameplay add-on purchased for extra bonus game opportunities, enhanced prize experiences, collectible game materials, and additional interactive challenges throughout the show.
  • The Party Upgrade Package cannot win, and does not affect, the purple Vacation Bingo prize in any way. The purple Vacation Bingo game is played exclusively on free purple cards.
  • Party Upgrade Package rounds award small promotional prizes only (for example: merch, sponsor swag, or novelty items).
  • The Party Upgrade Package may serve as one of several qualifying routes for stage-game contestant selection, alongside free routes (see Section 4). Free routes are always available.
  • The Party Upgrade Package is not a lottery ticket, raffle ticket, or wager, and has no cash value.

4. Stage Games — Skill-Based Contest Rules

  • Stage-game prizes are won through actual skill games — contests of performance, knowledge, or ability such as trivia, dance-offs, lyric challenges, and physical or creative challenges.
  • Rules and scoring are announced before each game. Winners are determined by scoring, performance, or judging — not by chance.
  • Contestants may be selected from any of the following routes:
    • Free WINNERS CIRCLE "Be On Our Game Shows" applications
    • Volunteers at the event
    • Host and audience participation moments
    • Skill qualifiers during the show
    • Party Upgrade Package qualifiers
  • No purchase is required to become a stage-game contestant — free selection routes are always available at every event featuring stage games.
  • Judging decisions of the host and designated judges are final.

5. WINNERS CIRCLE — Free Membership Program

  • WINNERS CIRCLE membership is free. Joining never requires a purchase. Join here.
  • Comp-entry passes. Members may receive free complimentary entry passes to eligible events. Comp entries admit the member to the same entertainment experience as ticketed guests, including eligibility for the free purple Vacation Bingo card.
  • Free Party Upgrade Package. Members may receive one free Party Upgrade Package at eligible events, with the same gameplay uses as a purchased Party Upgrade Package.
  • Comp-entry passes and free Party Upgrade Packages are promotional, subject to availability and event capacity, non-transferable unless stated otherwise, and have no cash value.
  • Membership benefits may vary by event and may change; eligible events are identified in member communications and event pages.

6. Sponsor Prizes & Certificates

  • Some prizes — including travel and vacation certificates — are provided by third-party sponsors and partners.
  • Sponsor-provided prizes and certificates are subject to the sponsor's own terms and conditions, which may include activation steps, reservation requirements, availability windows, blackout dates, taxes, fees, and other restrictions set by the sponsor.
  • Rave Bingo and IRL Events are not the issuer of sponsor certificates and are not responsible for sponsor fulfillment, availability, or changes to sponsor terms.
  • Prizes are not redeemable for cash unless expressly stated. Winners are responsible for any applicable taxes.

7. Global Disclaimers

  • NO PURCHASE NECESSARY to enter or win any random-draw or sweepstakes promotion. A purchase does not increase your chances of winning.
  • Not gambling. Rave Bingo events involve no wagering, no betting, no cash bingo, and no games where guests pay for a chance to win money.
  • Void where prohibited or restricted by law. Promotions are subject to all applicable federal, state, and local laws.
  • Guests must meet venue age and admission requirements (venues may be 18+ or 21+).
  • Rave Bingo / IRL Events may amend per-event rules to comply with local law; the rules announced or posted at each event control for that event.
  • Questions? Contact bookings@ravebingochi.com.
